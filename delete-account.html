Delete your FamPad account

FamPad, by Aparajita Sahay

Option 1: in the app (immediate)

  1. Open FamPad and sign in.
  2. Tap the account icon at the top of the Home screen.
  3. Tap Delete my account, enter your password if asked, and confirm.

Option 2: by email

If you can't use the app, email aparajita.sahay87@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my FamPad account", from the email address you signed up with. We delete the account within 30 days and confirm by email.

What is deleted

  • Your sign-in account (email address and password).
  • Your membership in every list, including your email address in those lists.
  • Every list that only you belonged to, with all its items, members and invites.
  • Your monthly scan count.

What is kept, and why

  • Lists you share with other people stay, with all their items, so they don't lose their family's plans. Your name is removed from them: items you added show as added by a deleted user.
  • If you were a shared list's admin, the member who joined first becomes its admin.
  • Photos are never stored, so there are none to delete.

Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. Shared lists can still be deleted by their remaining members.
