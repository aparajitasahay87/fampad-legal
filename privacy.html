FamPad Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 23, 2026

FamPad helps families keep shared lists of events, to-dos, checklists and notes. You can type items, or take a photo of a flyer, schedule or handwritten list and FamPad reads it for you. This policy explains what FamPad collects, why, who processes it, and how to delete it.

In short: we collect only what the app needs to work. We do not sell your data, show ads, or track you across other apps or websites. Photos you scan are read and then discarded by FamPad, never stored (see section 2).

1. What we collect

DataWhy
Email address and passwordTo create your account and sign you in. Your password is handled by Google Firebase Authentication. FamPad never sees or stores it.
Your lists and their items: titles, dates, times, places, children's first names you enter, fees, checklists and notesTo show your lists to you and to the people you share them with.
List membership: who is in each list, their role (admin, editor, viewer) and email addressSo the members of a list can see who shares it, and so only the right people can change it.
Invite codesSo people can join a list you share with them.
Number of scans this monthTo apply the monthly scan limit.
Photos you choose to scanTo read the text in them and turn it into items. See section 2.

FamPad does not collect your location, contacts, microphone, advertising ID or browsing history. It uses the camera and photo library only when you choose to take or pick a photo.

2. Photos and AI

When you scan a photo, it is sent over an encrypted connection to FamPad's server, which asks Google's Gemini AI to read it and return the events, to-dos and notes it finds. You review the result before anything is saved.

  • The photo exists only while it is being read. FamPad never stores your photos.
  • Google processes the photo under the Gemini API Additional Terms of Service. FamPad uses Google's paid Gemini API, under which Google does not use your photos or the results to improve its products. Google may keep them for a limited time only to detect abuse and meet legal requirements.
  • Before your first scan, the app asks for your permission to send photos to the AI.
  • Please don't scan things that you don't want processed this way, such as ID documents or medical records.

3. Who processes your data

FamPad runs on Google services: Firebase Authentication (sign-in), Cloud Firestore (your lists), Cloud Run (the server that reads photos), and the Gemini API (reading photos). Your data is stored on Google Cloud servers and encrypted in transit and at rest. We do not share your data with anyone else, except where the law requires it.

4. Who can see your lists

Only you and the people you invite to a list. Each person's role decides what they can do: admins manage members, editors add and change items, viewers can only look. Members of a list can see each other's email addresses.

5. How long we keep data

We keep your account and lists until you delete them or delete your account. FamPad never keeps photos; Google may keep them briefly only to detect abuse (section 2). Scan counts are kept per month.

6. Deleting your account and data

In the app, go to Account → Delete my account. You can also ask us by email. See how to delete your FamPad account for what is deleted and what is kept.

7. Children

FamPad is meant for parents and other adults who organize family life. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. Parents may enter their children's first names in items, and they can edit or delete them at any time.

8. Your rights

You can see, change and delete your data in the app at any time. Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or CCPA), you may have further rights, such as asking for a copy of your data. Email us and we will reply within 30 days.

9. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top and, for important changes, tell you in the app.
