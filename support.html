Report a problem or inappropriate content

Lists in FamPad are private and shared only with people you invite. If someone in a shared list adds something inappropriate or bothers you:

  • Leave the list: open the list, then Members & invites → Leave this list.
  • If you are the list's admin: remove that person in Members & invites, and delete the item.
  • Tell us: in the app, go to Account → Report a problem, or email us. Include the list name and what happened. We review every report within 24 hours and can remove content or accounts that break our rules.

Common questions

How do I share a list with my family?
Open the list → Members & invites → create an invite code and send it. They tap Join with a code on their Home screen.

What can I scan?
Paper flyers, invites, schedules and lists, or screenshots of chats and emails. Each photo uses one scan; scans reset on the 1st of each month.

Why do I need to verify my email?
So every family member is a real person. Open the link we email you once; after that you just sign in.

How do reminders work?
FamPad can remind you on the morning of an event and before it starts. Turn reminders on or off in Account → Reminders.

How do I delete my account?
Account → Delete my account. See what is deleted.
